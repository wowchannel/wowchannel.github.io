WOW Uploader

WOW Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the WOW YouTube channels ("WOW : The Wonders of the World" and its language versions in Korean, English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese).

It uploads videos that the owner made to the owner's own YouTube channels and schedules their publish time, using the YouTube Data API v3 (scope: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).

  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It only accesses the owner's own channels.
  • It does not read, collect or share any other person's data.
