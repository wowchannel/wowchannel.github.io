Privacy Policy — WOW Uploader

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What the app does

WOW Uploader is an internal tool used only by the owner of the WOW YouTube channels to upload their own videos and set their publish schedule through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube API to: upload videos with their title, description and tags; read the owner's channel ID and the list of the owner's uploaded videos to check the right channel was selected and to avoid duplicate uploads.

Storage

OAuth tokens and upload results are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. The app has no users other than the owner.

Google API Services

WOW Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files also removes all stored data.
